This is live, and I didn't touch it.

I built a model that plays out the rest of the Premier League season twenty thousand times and counts who won. Each bar below is a club's chance of winning the league. Arsenal at 36% means Arsenal finished top in about 36% of those simulated seasons.

The numbers are read straight from the model's own output file, which it rewrites by itself after every round of matches. If they've changed since you last looked, that's the model, not me.
