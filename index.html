Why this page exists

A product manager should be able to show you the thing.

Most of my professional work sits behind client NDAs and inside broadcast graphics you've seen without knowing whose feed drove them. That's a hard thing to prove on a résumé, so I stopped trying to.

Instead, everything on this site that carries a number is either something you can click and watch running, or something whose backtest script is in a public repository next to it. Where a model of mine failed, the failed version is still in the code and the results are on the page.
